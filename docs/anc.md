# $ANC

$ANC pays for Arcan. There is no venture capital in the project. Development is funded by trading fees on $ANC, and that is its major use case.

The confidential token standard is a separate thing. ERC-7984 seals amounts. $ANC does not. $ANC is the fee stream that keeps the protocol moving without a fund taking a stake.

When $ANC trades, the fees from that trading go back into building: the contracts, the coprocessors, and the SDK. The work stays funded by the people who use it.
